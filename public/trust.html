Trust

Plain answers about your data.

An AI that talks to your customers has to earn that. Here is what it does with their information, in one sentence each.

Who can see our customers' information?

Your team, the AIfy team that runs your setup, and the service providers listed below. Nobody else.

Is our data used to train AI models?

No. We don't use your conversations to train AI models, and our AI provider's commercial terms don't permit it to train on our API data by default.

Do you sell or share data for marketing?

No. We do not sell personal information, and we don't use your customers' details to market anything.

Do callers know they're talking to an AI?

Yes. The assistant says it's an AI at the start of the call, and it tells callers when a call is transcribed.

What happens when the AI isn't sure?

It hands off. Anything sensitive, a complaint, or a caller who asks for a person goes to your team.

Can we see what it said?

Yes. Every call and chat leaves a transcript and a short summary your team can read.

Can we approve messages before they go out?

Yes. Follow-up messages can be held as drafts until someone approves them. You choose which jobs run on their own.

Who owns the phone number, website and data?

You do. We explain where everything is stored before launch, and you can export your data at any time.

What does it cost, and are we locked in?

A fixed price to build, and a monthly plan for services that keep running. Both are in writing before we start.

Who do we talk to when something needs fixing?

The people who built your setup. Every message to info@aify.solutions reaches them directly.

The rules we follow

We handle personal information in line with Canada's PIPEDA and British Columbia's PIPA. Our AI only calls numbers that asked for a call, and we keep a do-not-call list.
