About this demo

Is that a real AI?

Yes. Nothing is scripted: it understands what you say, decides what to do, and uses the same kind of tools it would use in your store. Only the store, its schedule and its prices are samples.

How is it different in a real store?

It answers your real phone number, books into your scheduler where your systems allow it, texts customers from your number, and puts notes in your advisors' queue. We set the greeting, hours, prices and rules with you.

What happens with what I say here?

Your words are sent to the AI so it can answer, and we don't save demo conversations. Nothing reaches our team unless you fill in the form. Please don't share personal details in the demo.

Does it work for other businesses?

Yes. Clinics, trades, salons and service businesses use the same pattern: answer, book, confirm, brief the team. See all solutions.
