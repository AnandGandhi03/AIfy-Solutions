Questions dealers ask

Does it work with our scheduler and DMS?

We check your systems on the first call. Where direct booking is possible we connect to it; where it isn't, the agent captures the request and your team confirms.

What if a caller wants a person?

It transfers them, or takes a message for the right advisor when nobody is in. Anything it's unsure about goes to a person.

Can we control what it says?

Yes. You approve the greeting, the booking rules, the hours it works and the topics it won't discuss.

How long does it take to go live?

An after-hours pilot is the quickest start. The timeline depends on which integrations your store has enabled.
