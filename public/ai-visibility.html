Questions about GEO and AEO

What's the difference between GEO and AEO?

Very little. GEO (generative engine optimisation) and AEO (answer engine optimisation) both mean making your business easy for AI assistants to find, trust and recommend. We use both names for the same work.

Is this the same as what ChatGPT would say?

It's close, not identical. Every assistant searches and words things differently, and answers change over time. This free check is a quick snapshot; our full audit tests ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews.

What does fixing it involve?

Usually: consistent listings, a clear page for each service, structured data, a steady flow of genuine reviews, and a few genuinely useful articles. No content mills or spammy links. Read our GEO guide.

Do you guarantee results?

No one can honestly guarantee what an AI will say. We show you where you stand, fix what's in your control, and track how often you're mentioned every month.
