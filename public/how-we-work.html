Common questions

How long does a project take?

Most projects go live within a few weeks. The proposal gives you the exact timeline, which mostly depends on the tools we connect to.

A fixed price to build, and a monthly plan for services that keep running, like an AI receptionist. Both are in writing before we start.

Will it work with the tools we already use?

Usually. We check your calendar, CRM and other systems on the first call and tell you plainly what can connect.

Do you only work in BC?

No. We're based in Surrey, BC and work with businesses anywhere, fully remote.

What happens after launch?

We review real conversations and results with you, and keep improving what it says and does.
